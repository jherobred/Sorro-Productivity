# Basedly
Student workspace for desktop browsers, Android (Capacitor), and Xiaomi Pad 6 Pro with stylus.
Primary jobs: organize mixed study pages inside notebooks, plan tasks/calendar, maintain habits, gym, food, money, and focus.
Confirmed September 12: bold event-poster library/dashboard, calm editors, full light/dark support. Each notebook holds documents, journals, code, moodboards, infinite whiteboards, checklists, flashcards, Cornell notes, and PDF annotation including space outside page margins. Camera cutouts personalize notebook and task covers. No onboarding, buddy, community, messaging, files, projects, or profile routes in active navigation.
Preserve existing content and Supabase offline mutations. Photos remain local during background removal. Native stylus interaction uses Pointer Events, pressure and palm rejection.
